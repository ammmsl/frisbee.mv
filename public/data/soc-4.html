Denominator label — this page counts the never-joined periphery. Re-analysed in full on the presence substrate (2026-07-11): of the 150 who played but never joined the roster, 130 (87%) played exactly once and 81% never move past presocialization — the earlier first-timer-log figure (~81%) was not a measurement artifact (only 20 of 150 played 2+ on either substrate). The other headline, ~50% one-and-done (PB-C2), uses a different denominator: all newcomers, including those who joined the roster and became regulars (median ~7 plays each). Both are correct at their own denominator; never quote one as the other.
Provenance — the widened introducer trace (2026-07-11, T07) is PUBLIC chat-derived proxy evidence, not coded ground truth. Two signals: proxy-RSVP first-add (sign-up lists are copy-pasted forward, so the sender of the message where a name first appears is who signed them up — when that sender isn't them) and group-add (WhatsApp "X added Y" system messages). Across the whole chat population these yield 266 + 105 adder edges (160 + 34 strong after demoting high-volume adders >10 attributions and bulk pastes >3 new names — administrative intake, not recruitment). A first-add is an association proxy, not confirmed recruitment: where both signals exist for the same person (75 people), they name the same introducer only 36% of the time (44% where both are strong) — one friend may add you to the group, another may sign you up. Read it as "who plausibly brought them in", not a recruitment register. No owner-coded columns are used.

Findings
  • 122 of 150 (81%) never get past presocialization — a single trial appearance with no tenure, no chat and no one recorded introducing them. The periphery is overwhelmingly one-and-done (87% played exactly once on the Actual-Attendees substrate) — of the never-joined periphery; the all-newcomers figure is ~50% (PB-C2, different denominator).
  • Only 10 people reach socialization or near-insider acceptance without ever joining the paying roster — the genuine "regulars who never signed up" the roster misses.
  • 12 had a real spell then went quiet (lapsed >180 days) — visible drop-outs the club could have re-engaged.
  • The recruitment story is no longer dark. Widening the introducer trace with two PUBLIC chat signals (who first signed them onto a sign-up list; who added them to the group) traces 122 of 150 to a plausible introducer — vs 5 under first-@-mention alone — and 82 carry a strong (non-administrative, non-bulk) signal. Recruitment is peer-distributed: the top strong introducers are rank-and-file members, and committee leaders account for only 2 of the 82 strong attributions — the periphery arrives through friends, not officials. (Association proxy, not confirmed recruitment — see the provenance caveat.)
Method, data & caveats
Each of the 150 (engagement='player') is placed on Donnelly & Young's socialization stages via ordinal thresholds on play count, tenure (first→last activity), message activity, and whether anyone @-mentioned them. Play count = presence substrate (gS_rsvp.presence(): Session Input "Actual Attendees" person×session, 99.4% resolution — the substrate that covers off-roster repeat play directly), with new_players as fallback for the 4 people never on an attendee list; the presence span also widens tenure where it exceeds the chat-derived span. Rules: presocialization = 1 play, 0 tenure, no talk, unnamed; selection = played again or engaged/introduced; socialization = ≥3 plays with ≥30-day tenure; acceptance = ≥5 plays + ≥30-day tenure + messages; exit = a real spell then lapsed >180 days (stage rules use the original first-@-mention signal only, so the T06 stage distribution is unaffected by the trace widening). Introducer trace (widened 2026-07-11, T07): two PUBLIC chat-derived signals — proxy-RSVP first-add (sender of the sign-up-list message where the person's name first ever appears, sender ≠ them; lists are copy-pasted forward so first occurrence = the add) and group-add (membership_events "X added Y"), with first-@-mention as last-resort fallback. Precedence: strong proxy-RSVP > strong group-add > bulk/admin variants > mention. [ASSUMPTION] adders with >10 attributions and list messages introducing >3 never-seen names are administrative intake, demoted to weak. Between-signal agreement where both exist: 27/75 (7/16 strong-both) — an association proxy, not confirmed recruitment. No owner-coded columns read. [ASSUMPTION] thresholds are analyst-set ordinal cut-offs (not observed stage transitions); [ASSUMPTION] unresolved attendee names keep stable raw keys, a small bias toward one-and-done. Descriptive. No names emitted. Script: analysis/gB_soc4.py on analysis/gB_panel.py + analysis/gS_rsvp.py. [Interpretation limitation, internally verified] A portion of the never-joined periphery one-timers are short-stay visitors — travelling players and novelty drop-ins who were never convertible — so the count of genuine conversion failures is smaller than the presocialization share implies. The adjustment falls on the count, not the rate (visitors leave after one play at about the periphery's base rate).
