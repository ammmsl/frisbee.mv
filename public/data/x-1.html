The one-paragraph diagnosis

UFA is an emerging-sport volunteer body that acquired a formal federation shell years ahead of its lived practice. Coercive isomorphism — the Commissioner-of-Sports registration template plus WFDF alignment — handed it a Boardroom charter in 2024, while the organisation on the ground is still a founder-run kitchen table playing pickup on one rented pitch. The shell is real legitimacy (registration, WFDF membership, elections, an ethics committee) but thin operationally: one indispensable treasurer and a single un-contracted venue. Its financial controls are appropriately staged — the annual Ministry submission sets the financial-year cycle and a formal audit + co-signatory are deferred to National-Association status by design. Its deepest risk is Horch's paradox — the very formalisation that earns legitimacy pulls it toward the audited, professional, national-team structures of the NA milestone, which its volunteer core cannot yet staff, and over-doing it could erode the spirit-of-the-game solidarity that is its actual glue.

The fault lines every lens keeps hitting
Findings
  • The shell arrived before the substance. Registration (2024) and WFDF membership (2025) gave UFA a formal federation identity in six years — but every operational lens (Mintzberg, Thompson, decoupling) finds a volunteer kitchen-table underneath. The gap is the whole story.
  • Ideology, not structure, is what coordinates UFA. With a negligible technostructure and a tiny apex, it runs on the spirit-of-the-game and a handful of founders' goodwill — a Missionary configuration. That is a strength (cheap, cohesive) and the exact thing formalisation can corrode.
  • Horch's paradox is the central tension. External legitimacy (WFDF/Ministry) pulls toward professional structures; the volunteer core can't yet staff them, and paying/auditing its way to 'proper' governance risks crowding out the volunteer energy. The patronage-fee model and spirit-of-game norms are the current bulwarks against that trap.
  • Three fault lines recur under every lens: single-venue dependence (un-buffered, RDT), the operational treasurer bus-factor of 1 (350 people lean on one office — the audit/co-signatory are deferred to the NA milestone by design, and the concentration is mitigated by the single-purpose account + annual Ministry submission), and founder succession (a closed bloc with a veto and a 5-year term). The near-term risk is the venue and the operational treasurer concentration; the audit/financial-year formalisation arrives with NA status.
