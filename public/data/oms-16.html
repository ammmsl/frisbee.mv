The anchor — why real placement stays kitchen-table
Findings
  • UFA's formal placement has climbed the full continuum in six years — from a 2018 kitchen-table WhatsApp group (position 0) to a 2025 Boardroom shell (~1.0): registered, 9 offices, 5 committees, two STV-elected AGMs and WFDF affiliation.
  • That climb was template-driven, not organically grown: the 2024 jump came from adopting the Commissioner-of-Sports registration boilerplate wholesale, so the Boardroom form arrived ahead of a Boardroom's practice.
  • Real, lived placement sits far lower (~0.35) — near the kitchen table — because an entrenched founders' bloc holds a unanimous veto over any committee decision, gatekeeps the Elections Board, and can block amendments. Elected organs sit under a closed-class veto, so authority never truly disperses.
  • The unusually long 5-year committee term compounds this: a Boardroom rotates its board often; UFA locks it for five years, further concentrating power and slowing the shell from filling with Boardroom practice.
  • [ASSUMPTION] Positions on the 0–2 continuum are the run's document-coded judgement of direction, not a validated instrument. Verdict: a formalised Boardroom shell over a volunteer core — nowhere near Executive Office (no paid staff), and the founders' veto is what keeps the real placement kitchen-table.
Method, data & caveats
Framework. Kikulis, Slack & Hinings (1992) design archetypes for voluntary sport organisations, read as a lifecycle placement (the L3 sibling of TB-1, which scored the dimensions). Continuum: Kitchen Table (0) (volunteer, informal, private) → Boardroom (1) (formal elected board, standardised, mixed volunteer/professional) → Executive Office (2) (professional-staff-led). Method: place each governance milestone (GD.MILESTONES) on the continuum to trace the formal trajectory, then mark the lived placement using the entrenched founder powers (GD.FOUNDER_POWERS), offices (GD.OFFICES) and the 5-year term (GD.COMMITTEE_TERM_YEARS) as the counterweight.
