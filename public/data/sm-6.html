Scoring the application against the 7 WFDF criteria
The path from Provisional to full
Findings
  • UFA is largely WFDF-ready: 6 of 7 criteria are met — non-discrimination, ≥50 disc-playing members, an AGM/election mechanism, dues, good standing, and annual Census reporting. Provisional NF membership was granted Feb 2025.
  • The single partial is ‘fairly represent all disc games’: UFA is the sole disc body, but activity is ~entirely Ultimate — Dodgedisc was only just added in 2025. To satisfy the letter of the criterion the body must evidence genuine breadth across disc sports, not just Ultimate.
  • Annual Census reporting is now met: the obligation was accepted in the application and the annual return is filed with WFDF (owner-confirmed 2026-07) — the earlier "no artifact in the archive" gap is closed.
  • So the gap-to-full is narrow and administrative: broaden verified activity beyond Ultimate (the Census is now filed). Neither requires new governance organs — the elected board, constitution and dues are all in place. [ASSUMPTION] statuses are the run's document-coded reading of the application, not WFDF's own adjudication.
  • Read against the maturity story (TB-1/OMS-16), WFDF-readiness is ahead of UFA's lived organisational maturity — the formal shell clears most international criteria while the volunteer core is still kitchen-table. Regulative legitimacy is running ahead of practice.
Method, data & caveats
Framework. Gap analysis against WFDF's National-Federation membership criteria — the seven obligations in the Provisional-NF application letter (GD.WFDF_CRITERIA), each a 4-tuple (criterion, requirement, status, evidence). Statuses are the run's document-coded assessment on a three-level scale: met (evidenced in the application), partial (present but incomplete), assumed (obligation accepted but no supporting artifact in the archive). The score table colours status with the design system's semantic tokens (--good/--warn/--bad). The timeline reads the one remaining non-met item (all-games representation) as the gap-to-full. Sources (gD_evidence, PUBLIC + INTERNAL-aggregate): the WFDF NF application (6 parts + active-players list + budget), the Provisional-NF letter, and the Articles of Association; no RESTRICTED data; office-holders as roles only. Document-coded, not statistical, and not WFDF's own adjudication. Script: analysis/gD_sm6.py; substrate: analysis/gD_evidence.py.
