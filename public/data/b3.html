Findings
  • UFA's response to competing institutional demands is uniformly low-resistance: of 7 demand-sources, 2 acquiescence and 5 compromise — and zero defiance or manipulation. A small, young, resource-dependent body has little leverage to do anything else.
  • The state and the international federation get pure acquiescence: UFA adopted the Commissioner-of-Sports registration template wholesale (even carrying the un-personalised WFDF letter boilerplate) and baked WFDF rules + spirit-of-the-game straight into its charter. This is ceremonial-form adoption — legitimacy by conformity.
  • The resource-holders get compromise: the Ministry (field access), the City Council (a bookable pitch), and sponsors are all courted by formal letter while UFA adapts its schedule and model to what they'll grant — balancing its needs against their rules rather than confronting them.
  • The one internal competing demand — a cost-recovery fee vs. members' willingness-to-pay — is also resolved by compromise: an AGM-set patronage price (100 reg + membership) that splits the difference. Even the WFDF ‘all disc games’ demand is met by gradual compromise (Dodgedisc added 2025), not full conformance.
  • [ASSUMPTION] Classifications are the run's document-coded reading of correspondence and the charter, not observed negotiation transcripts. The pattern is coherent with Pache & Santos: an organisation this small and dependent manages competing demands by conforming, not by defying or manipulating — resistance would risk the very legitimacy and resources it is courting.
Method, data & caveats
Framework. Oliver (1991) strategic responses to institutional processes — a five-point continuum of acquiescence → compromise → avoidance → defiance → manipulation (increasing active resistance) — read through Pache & Santos (2010) on how organisations facing competing institutional demands respond, with attention to size and resource-dependence. Method: enumerate UFA's institutional demand-sources from GD.CORRESPONDENCE, GD.WFDF_CRITERIA and GD.EVIDENCE; classify the response to each and cite the evidence; tally the mix. Sources (gD_evidence, PUBLIC + INTERNAL-aggregate): Ministry letter 02/2025, FVM City Council letter 01/2025, the field-access proposal, the WFDF NF application + Provisional letter, sponsor letter, and the Articles of Association; no RESTRICTED data; office-holders as roles only.
