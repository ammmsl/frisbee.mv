Findings
  • UFA scores 7/12 (58%) on the six-dimension good-governance self-audit — a solid pass on the paper machinery and on financial accountability, held down only on checks & balances. It is far better governed than an informal pickup club and, for a pre-NA association, its financial controls are appropriately staged.
  • The strengths are real and enacted: a written constitution, an AGM with STV elections actually run in 2024 and 2025, an annual + financial report tabled, an Ethics Committee, and 2 of 4 women on the exec. Two dimensions score a full 2 — solidarity/inclusivity (open objectives + a fee-free junior class) and accountability (an externally imposed financial-year cycle, with a formal audit staged to NA status).
  • Accountability over money is present, not absent: the financial-year reporting cycle is externally imposed by the mandatory annual post-AGM Ministry / COS submission, and a formal external audit is staged to National-Association status by design of the COS template (FINANCE_STAGED) — it arrives with NA, not pre-NA. The one residual is operational: treasurer-function concentration, mitigated by the single-purpose account + the annual submission (FINANCE_RESIDUAL).
  • Checks & balances scores 0 — the one absent dimension. The founders' unanimous veto sits above the elected board and the 5-year term is long, so democratic process is partly overridden by design (FOUNDER_POWERS). The check and the checked are the same core (see the TB-2 conflation reading).
  • [ASSUMPTION] Scores are the run's 0–2 document-coded judgement against a reduced BIBGIS/AGGIS checklist, not a certified audit; they express where UFA is strong and where it is exposed, not an accredited rating. A formal governance review + an audited financial statement would convert several 1s toward 2s.
Method, data & caveats
Framework. A national-sport-governance good-governance checklist in the BIBGIS / AGGIS tradition (Basic / Advanced Indicators of Better Governance in Sport; Geeraert et al. / Play the Game), reduced to six dimensions — transparency, democratic process, accountability, checks-and-balances, solidarity/inclusivity, integrity. Each is scored 0 (absent) / 1 (partial) / 2 (present) against the Articles of Association and AGM 2024/2025 records via GD.score_table. Strong marks: written constitution, STV elections (GD.EVIDENCE), AGM reporting, Ethics Committee, 2/4 women at exec, and financial accountability — a financial-year cycle imposed by the annual Ministry / COS submission (GD.FINANCE_HAS) with a formal audit staged to NA status (GD.FINANCE_STAGED). Weak mark: checks & balances, from the founders' veto (GD.FOUNDER_POWERS) + the 5-year term. Total 7/12 (58%). Sources: PUBLIC + INTERNAL-aggregate; no RESTRICTED data; office-holders as roles only.
