Findings
  • UFA has cleanly traversed Greiner Phase 1 (Creativity) — informal WhatsApp-organised play from 2018 — into Phase 2 (Direction), marked by the 2024 session logging and 2024-07 payment ledger: a small committee now directs operations.
  • The Phase-1→2 transition resolved a crisis of leadership: the 2025 move to a 4-role committee + a set fee (51 members) is exactly the professional-management response Greiner predicts.
  • UFA is now in a late Direction phase and founder-centric — run day-to-day by President + Treasurer, all records held by one role. The next predicted crisis is AUTONOMY: as sessions and money scale, mid-level organisers come to need decision rights the current structure doesn't grant.
  • The 2026 fee restructure (to a 250 MVR patronage price) and the second paid venue are Direction-phase fine-tuning, not a phase change — they optimise the existing centralised model rather than delegate it. Watch for a move to multiple venue/finance leads as the autonomy tell.
