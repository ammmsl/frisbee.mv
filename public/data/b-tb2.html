Findings
  • UFA is a formalising single-city club (stage 3 of 5) — well past informal grassroots, but far from the national-federation stage where UPA/USA Ultimate, UK Ultimate and WFTDA sit.
  • Its ~56-member scale matches these bodies' earliest years, not their mature size — the fair comparison is to their founding decade, when they too were volunteer-run with modest dues.
  • UFA's 7-year informal runway (2018→2025) before formalising is longer than the fast-formalising modern bodies (USQ formalised within a few years) — closer to the slow, organic ultimate/roller-derby pattern.
  • The USQ cautionary curve (rapid rise then contraction/merger) is the relevant risk: emerging sports that formalise faster than their base can sustain decline. UFA's slower, membership-funded path looks more like the durable volunteer-federation model. [≈] all comparator claims approximate.
