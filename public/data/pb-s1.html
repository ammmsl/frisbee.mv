How big is a pickup session, and when does the game fire?

Part A found no enumerated session-size distributions and no measured game-on thresholds for pickup sport (literature gaps #1–2). This page benchmarks UFA's 215 tracked Villingili sessions against the 10–14 sign-up heuristic and the declared 12-player quorum, and extends the size series back through the untracked 2021–2023 era via chat sign-up lists.

Findings
  • The enumerated distribution the literature lacks: a Villingili pickup session runs at a median 22 players (mean 22.8, range 11–41, n=215) — roughly double the 10–14 "game-on" heuristic and comfortably above the 14 needed for 7v7. Part A's session minima describe survival; this community operates at saturation of a full-size pitch.
  • The declared quorum is real, and so — rarely — is failure: only 1 of 215 tracked sessions fired below the 12-player minimum (an 11), and the censored side is now measured: across ~2.5 years, 4 announced sessions were genuinely cancelled — two demand failures (Granovetter threshold failures observed in the wild: 13 sign-ups vs a ~20 requirement in Jan 2025; 8 vs the 12 quorum in May 2026, called in so many words — "we don't have enough people tonight") and two venue losses (bumped by another booking; pre-empted for Eid prayer prep — B4's tertiary-customer exposure in both its commercial and community forms). A ~1.6% attempt-failure rate, split evenly between demand and venue: at a ~22-player median the threshold rarely binds, and when it does it is at the margins — holiday weeks and non-standard booking configurations.
  • The blackout era ran below today's quorum: chat-derived sign-up medians were ~9–10 in 2021 and ~8 in 2022 — at or under the modern 12-player minimum — rising to ~11–12 across 2023 and ~22 in the tracked era. The community crossed its own critical-mass threshold in 2023–24, coincident with the move to paid evening turf (association, not causation; the free-era sessions had no quorum to clear).
  • Size follows the pitch: futsal venues run ~16–17 players, the FAM-morning era ~15, Villingili ~23 — consistent with small-sided-games logic and correction #1 (venue, not format, is the variable).
  • A second sport surfaced in the corpus: the dodge-disc (Dodgebee) dates (Central Park, from late 2025) are not a pickup variant but a new sport entirely — introduced at the association's first anniversary under its WFDF-facing mandate as the Maldives Flying Disc Federation (all disc sports, by design). Out of scope for pickup benchmarks; it is the federation mandate's first test case (a school-introduction programme with 50 donated discs is pending).
Method, data & caveats
Session sizes from sessions.real_pax (the 3 free member-event rows with real_pax≤1 are excluded per the owner's classification). Venues normalised via corrections.venue(). The 2021–2023 series is reconstructed from numbered sign-up lists in the chat (analysis/gS_rsvp.py): a message is a sign-up list if it has ≥4 numbered name-like entries; its session date is parsed from the header (day+month, year inferred to within [−2, +21] days of posting); per day the largest active list is taken, monthly medians shown. Sign-up size is a proxy for headcount (PB-S2 measures the no-show/walk-on flows that separate them). Untracked announced dates: header dates parse from copy-pasted lists in which errors propagate (owner), so the DB session date is authoritative — any announced date with no session row merges onto the nearest session within ±3 days unless the founders' admin chat / owner (INTERNAL; cited as classification only) marks it a real cancellation or a separate event. Of 35: 24 slips merged, 4 cancellations (2 quorum, 2 venue), 1 training day, 3 Dodge-bee, 1 off-tracker HDC, 2 unresolved. All figures aggregate; no names leave the gitignored inputs.
