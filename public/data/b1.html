Decoupling is not bad faith. Meyer & Rowan's point is that a young NGB must adopt the formal shell (a WFDF-aligned constitution, a national-team objective, a full committee chart) to be a legitimate national body at all — and then run a pickup club underneath it because that is what its people and resources allow. The gaps below are the cost of buying legitimacy early. The finding is where the shell and the practice have come apart — widest on the national-team / all-atoll objective. (Financial controls are a separate matter: the charter's silence on audit/signatory is external + staged to NA status by design of the COS template, not decoupling — see the finance row.)
Findings
  • Across 5 formal provisions, UFA shows the textbook decoupling pattern: a legitimacy-bearing formal shell (WFDF-aligned constitution, national-team objective, 9-office board, elected committee) running over a lived pickup club of ~350 people. The shell was formalised ahead of the practice.
  • The widest gap is means–ends: the AoA mandates grassroots → national teams → international success across all atolls, but the activity is recreational pickup plus a few tournaments in Greater Malé (Fuvahmulah/Addu only introduced). No standing national team is fielded — the mandate is a federation's, the practice is a Greater-Malé club's (quantified in OMS-5).
  • Financial controls are NOT a decoupling gap. The charter's silence on audit/financial-year/signatory is external + staged by design of the COS template (FINANCE_STAGED): the annual Ministry / COS submission already imposes the financial-year cycle, and a formal audit + co-signatory arrive with National-Association status. Money runs through a separate, single-purpose account; the only residual is the operational treasurer bus-factor, mitigated by that account + the submission (FINANCE_RESIDUAL).
  • The structure and the elected board are moderately decoupled: 9 offices exist on paper but 'players organise everything' (17 May 2025 debrief), and elections are genuinely run yet a founders' veto sits above the result. Gender parity is the narrowest gap — 2/4 women at exec is real, but no woman chairs a committee, so it is kept only at the apex.
  • [ASSUMPTION] Gap sizes are the run's document-coded judgement (narrow/moderate/wide), and the 'does Y' column is read from AGM records + the event debrief, not a field audit. The corroborating self-report conflicts (founding year, player counts, session counts) show the same organisation reporting ceremonial rather than reconciled numbers.
Method, data & caveats
Framework. Meyer & Rowan (1977) institutional decoupling — organisations adopt formal structures as legitimacy-bearing myths, then decouple them from actual work — sharpened by Bromley & Powell (2012) into policy–practice and means–ends decoupling. Each formal provision is coded into a says X / does Y / gap row from the substrate: financial controls from GD.FINANCE_HAS/FINANCE_STAGED/FINANCE_RESIDUAL (external + staged to NA status by design — not a decoupling gap); the national-team & all-atoll objective and the office/committee structure from GD.EVIDENCE/GD.OFFICES; the founders' veto from GD.FOUNDER_POWERS; the self-report conflicts from GD.SELF_REPORT_CONFLICTS; people reached (~350; roster 172, periphery 150) from corrections.COUNTS (deduped). Gap size is a document-coded judgement (narrow/moderate/wide). Sources: PUBLIC + INTERNAL-aggregate; no RESTRICTED data; office-holders and the treasurer as roles only. Companion: OMS-5 (DB-anchored). Descriptive, not causal. Script: analysis/gD_b1.py; substrate: analysis/gD_evidence.py, analysis/corrections.py.
