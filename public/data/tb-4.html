Findings
  • UFA spent ~7 years informal (2018–2024) — a WhatsApp chat with @-mention coordination and no records — before any systemic formalisation. The professionalisation path started late and moved fast.
  • The formalisation is front-loaded into 2024–2025: session records, the payment ledger and newcomer intake all appeared in 2024, then membership + fee + a 4-role committee in 2025 — the single biggest step (+2).
  • Growth is on the organisational and systemic axes; the occupational axis stays at zero — UFA is 100% volunteer, no paid staff. On Dowling-Nagel it is a structurally + systemically formalising, occupationally amateur club.
  • The 2026 fee restructure (to a 250 MVR patronage price) and the second paid venue are system-tuning, not new formalisation — the trajectory is plateauing at a 'lightly professionalised volunteer club' level, consistent with its small scale (56 members).
Method, data & caveats
Milestones are real DB dates: informal founding (min membership_events.date = 2018-09-28); @-mention coordination (2021-07-15); session records (2024-01-05); payment ledger (2024-07-02); newcomer intake (2024-08-09); membership+fee+committee (min reg_2025 = 2025-01-01); fee restructure + second venue (first 2026 Hulhumalé HDC session = 2026-05-18). Each event is coded to a Dowling-Nagel professionalisation axis (organisational / systemic / occupational) and given an ordinal +step (the 2025 membership+committee event = +2 as the largest jump). Cumulative score = running sum (8 total). [ASSUMPTION] axis tags and step weights are our framework coding, not measured; the score is an ordinal trajectory, not a validated index. [ASSUMPTION] occupational axis = 0 because the DB shows no paid roles (all volunteer). Corroborates OMS-1 (Greiner Direction phase) and TB's lifecycle placement. No individuals named.
