The vacancy mechanism vs the knowledge gap. The AoA does provide a way to refill a seat — vacancies go to the next AGM, and an Elections Board (with an emergency route) exists. That is legitimate succession of the office. What it does not provide is succession of the knowledge: no handover file, no shared ledger, no deputy Treasurer. A new person can be elected into the chair, but inherits an empty desk.
Findings
  • Mean succession-readiness is 1.1 of 3 — roles are documented but not transferable. All 9 of 9 offices have an AoA-defined duty, but only 1 has an actual deputy and 0 have their working knowledge codified.
  • The one office with a designed backup is the President (the Vice-President deputises). Every portfolio chair and the money role run on a single volunteer with tacit know-how — the 17 May debrief's 'players organising everything' is exactly this un-codified state.
  • Readiness is lowest on the money functions: the Treasurer scores 1/3 — documented duty, but no codified handover (a private ledger) and no deputy yet. This is an operational succession gap; the formal co-signer + audit are staged to National-Association status by design (GD.FINANCE_STAGED), and the annual Ministry submission already sets the financial-year cycle. The most critical role is the least knowledge-transferable today.
  • UFA has genuine office succession (next-AGM vacancy filling + an Elections Board) but almost no knowledge succession. A seat can be refilled; the incumbent's ledger, contacts and routines cannot — the successor inherits the title, not the working memory.
  • [ASSUMPTION] Codified/deputy are the run's document-coded reading of the AoA + the staged finance design + the debrief; a real handover file for any portfolio would flip that office's Codified bit. The scores express direction (documented-high, transferable-low), not a validated instrument.
Method, data & caveats
Framework. Succession-readiness / knowledge-transferability audit: for each office score three binary conditions — Documented (duty defined in the charter), Codified (working knowledge written rather than tacit), Deputy (a named backup/co-signer who can step in) — summed to a 0–3 readiness score. Sources (gD_evidence, PUBLIC + INTERNAL-aggregate): the Articles of Association office duties (GD.OFFICES), its vacancy-filling + Elections Board provisions, the finance design (GD.FINANCE_STAGED: a co-signatory + formal audit staged to NA status by design; GD.FINANCE_RESIDUAL: the residual is operational, mitigated by the single-purpose account + annual Ministry submission), and the 17 May 2025 event debrief for the tacit-knowledge reading. Rendered via GD.score_table. Office-holders as roles only; no RESTRICTED data; document-coded, not statistical. Script: analysis/gD_oms4.py; substrate: analysis/gD_evidence.py.
