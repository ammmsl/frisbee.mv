Findings
  • The @-network has a distinct dense core — a 12-core of 22 people — and a a clear core/periphery signature (Borgatti–Everett r=0.404).
  • Committee and founders occupy that core (median coreness 12 committee, 11 founders); roster members sit mid-shell (median 3) and the k≤1 rim is roster + chat-only stragglers.
  • The headline: the 150-strong periphery is not a k=1 shell — it is off the graph entirely. Only 8 of 150 played-not-on-roster people appear in the @-mention network; 142 are complete isolates. They play, but they are socially invisible in the club's talk.
  • [ASSUMPTION] k-core on the undirected, self-loop-free graph. The off-graph finding is the more important one — a k=1-shell test presupposes membership the periphery doesn't have.
Method, data & caveats
k-core decomposition (nx.core_number) on the undirected, self-loop-free @-mention graph (n=136). Continuous core/periphery fit = Pearson correlation between the observed adjacency and the ideal pattern c_i·c_j with c = coreness scaled to [0,1] (Borgatti–Everett style), r=0.404. The periphery cross-check joins people.engagement='player' to graph membership. Substrate analysis/gA_graph.py; script analysis/gA_nq5.py.
