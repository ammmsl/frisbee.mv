Findings
  • On paper UFA scores 1.2 — a Boardroom design: a registered NGB with nine offices, five committees, an elected board, a written constitution and WFDF alignment. Its formal shell is already well up the professionalisation curve.
  • In practice it scores 0.5 — a Kitchen Table: unpaid volunteers wearing many hats, an entrenched founders' bloc, one de-facto treasurer, and effectiveness judged by turnout. The lived organisation is still the kitchen table.
  • The 0.7-point gap is the whole story — UFA formalised its shell ahead of its practice. The widest gaps are domain (charter promises national teams; practice is pickup) and centralisation (elected board on paper; founder-concentrated in fact).
  • The one dimension that closes is standardisation — rules and election procedures are real, and money-control formalisation (audit / signatory) is deferred to the Ministry mandate / National-Association milestone by design of the COS template, so it is externally staged rather than absent. The annual Ministry submission already sets the financial-year cycle. This connects to OMS-7 (the NA milestone), B1 and OMS-5.
  • [ASSUMPTION] Scores are the run's document-coded judgement on a 0–2 continuum, not a validated instrument; they express direction and gap, not precision. A member survey would let the 'practice' column be triangulated rather than analyst-coded.
Method, data & caveats
Framework. Kikulis, Slack & Hinings (1992) design archetypes for voluntary sport organisations: Kitchen Table (volunteer, private, informal), Boardroom (formal board, standardised, mixed volunteer/professional), Executive Office (professional-staff-led, results-oriented). Six dimensions — three value (orientation, domain, effectiveness criteria) and three structural (specialisation, standardisation, centralisation) — each scored KT=0 / BR=1 / EO=2 twice: as the formal corpus designs it and as practice enacts it. Overall score = mean across dimensions. Sources (gD_evidence, PUBLIC + INTERNAL-aggregate): the Articles of Association / Hingaa Gavaidhu, AGM 2024/2025 records, the 17 May 2025 event-debrief notes; no RESTRICTED data; office-holders as roles only.
