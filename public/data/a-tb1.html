The pillar profile
Findings
  • UFA scores 10/27 on a SPLISS-lite pathway. The three pillars that reach emerging are participation (P3) — 10 events, 333 slots, 29% female, outer-island reach — competition (P8) — 4 tournaments plus League 2026 — and governance (P2) — a registered, elected, WFDF-recognised NGB. Demand and structure are the club's assets.
  • The coaching pillar (P7) is only nascent: one 5-day clinic with an international coach (GSP partnership) trained 17, with monthly training days — a valuable one-off injection, but no salaried coach and no certification pipeline, so it is not yet a coaching system.
  • Talent-ID (P4) rests entirely on a Hat-Seeding peer-rating sheet (~25 players) built to balance hat-draft teams — a team-balancing tool doubling as ID, not a development-squad pipeline. And facilities (P6) are weak: a single rented pitch with no booking rights or contract (the single-venue risk), no owned training base.
  • Two pillars are absent (0/3): athletic & post-career support (P5) and scientific research (P9) — expected for an amateur club, but they mean there is no performance-support layer at all. Financial support (P1) is nascent: solvent and debt-free, but no performance budget beyond fees + sponsorship.
  • [ASSUMPTION] Read against a success pathway, UFA has built the base of the pyramid (participation, competition, a governance shell) but not the development apparatus above it (coaching, facilities, talent-ID, science). Scores are the run's 0–3 document-coded reading, expressing profile and gaps, not a validated SPLISS measurement or an elite-readiness claim.
Method, data & caveats
Framework. SPLISS — Sports Policy factors Leading to International Sporting Success (De Bosscher et al.) — nine policy pillars: (1) financial support, (2) governance/organisation & structure, (3) sport participation, (4) talent identification & development, (5) athletic & post-career support, (6) training facilities, (7) coaching provision & development, (8) (inter)national competition, (9) scientific research. Run as SPLISS-lite: because UFA is a young single-sport NGB (not a nation), each pillar is scored 0 absent / 1 nascent / 2 emerging / 3 established for the resources UFA has actually built; overall = 10/27.
