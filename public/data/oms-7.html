The institutional field — who UFA answers to, and for what
The formalisation is a planned milestone — earning National-Association status
Findings
  • UFA sits inside a 6-actor institutional field with two distinct tie-types: statutory (the Commissioner of Sports as incorporator/registrar; WFDF as regulator) and resource (the Ministry, city councils, the venue owner, and sponsors). Legitimacy flows from the first pair; survival resources from the second.
  • Today those demands are light — file a report, court a letter, conform to rules — which is why UFA's response has been acquiescence + compromise (B3). The field is permissive because UFA is small and new; the shock is that it won't stay permissive.
  • The formalisation is a planned milestone, not a shock: moving Provisional→full NF and winning Ministry National-Association recognition raises the bar in lockstep — the annual Census filed (now done — owner-confirmed 2026-07), ≥50 verified members rather than the ~56 claimed, national-team fielding, a formal audit and a financial-year close. NA is a known, deliberate, multi-year goal to prepare for.
  • The audit + financial-year close are staged to NA status by design of the COS template (FINANCE_STAGED) — not a charter gap. The annual Ministry / COS submission already imposes the financial-year reporting cycle, money runs through a separate single-purpose account, and a formal audit + co-signatory arrive with NA. The one residual is the operational treasurer bus-factor, mitigated by that account + the submission (FINANCE_RESIDUAL; see OMS-16, B1).
  • The milestone is already scaffolded in the constitution: its forward clauses — Youth Development Programme (YDP), gender-based activity promotion, international-tournament participation — are seeded ahead of time (NA_PATHWAY). So the preparation is a scheduled upgrade UFA is already building toward: file the Census, verify the ≥50 register, and commission the audit as NA status comes into reach — a known step on a known path.
  • [ASSUMPTION] The milestone timing is a forecast, not a dated regulator demand — it reads the direction of travel from the milestones and criteria. The staging is by design: the audit / financial-year close / verified register arrive with NA status, and the near-term controls (annual submission + single-purpose account) are already in force.
Method, data & caveats
Framework. Institutional-field mapping (DiMaggio & Powell's organisational field; resource-dependence on the actors that supply legitimacy and resources), plus a directional shock forecast. Method: enumerate the external actors UFA depends on / answers to and code each one's relationship, UFA's obligation, and the tightening scenario, from GD.CORRESPONDENCE, GD.WFDF_CRITERIA, GD.MILESTONES and GD.EVIDENCE; then read the Provisional→full NF + NA-recognition step-up as a planned milestone, rendering what it stages by design (GD.FINANCE_STAGED) and the forward clauses already seeded in the constitution for it (GD.NA_PATHWAY).
