Findings
  • Gap #5 measured: the lapsed-player → engager pipeline barely exists here. Of 235 players inactive 90+ days, 83% vanish entirely; 26 (11%) keep chatting (median residue ~118 days, then silence), 9 volunteer, 6 become paying patrons. The industry assumption that ex-players convert into a fan/volunteer layer is not what this community's record shows — consistent with Part A's finding that fandom is recruited relationally, not from playing history.
  • The live second channel is return, not spectating: 41 of 317 players (13%) have already lapsed 6+ months and come back. A 'lapsed' list is therefore partly a future-player list — re-engagement campaigns have a measured base rate to beat, and Part A's review says re-engagement is exactly where participation effects actually appear.
  • A pure-supporter class exists but is small: 6 of 56 paid 2026 members (11%) haven't played at all in 2026 (10 at ≤2 sessions). The patronage model is doing what it was designed to do (OMS-6) — but at ~11% of membership, UFA is still a players' club, nowhere near the emerging-sport inversion (pickleball ~10 players per fan) flipping.
  • The volunteer economy is thin: 204 players in the last 12 months per 24 people with any admin trace — 8.5:1 at the most generous count, ~41:1 against the ~5-person real core — a thin coordination layer for a club this size. This is the bus-factor story (TB-5/OMS-4) expressed in Part A's units, and the single strongest structural strain anywhere in Part B. (We cite no national benchmark ratio: England-wide volunteering figures mix definitions of "participant" and "volunteer" and aren't measured at club level.)
  • [ASSUMPTION] Outcome categories are behavioural proxies on the tracked record: 'volunteer' = WhatsApp admin actions only (portfolio work like event organising is invisible here), 'silent' means no trace in this community's channels. The member survey (Part B instrument N1) is the designed triangulation.
Method, data & caveats
Universe = the 317 resolvable players in the presence substrate (gS_rsvp.presence(), deduped; raw-name-only appearances excluded). Lapsed = last presence ≥90 days before 2026-06-30. Outcomes require a signal ≥14 days after the last session: chat-engager = any later message; volunteer = any later membership_events action; patron = paid 2026 membership with last play before 2026; categories are exclusive in priority order volunteer > patron > chat-engager > silent. Returner = any two consecutive presences ≥180 days apart (computed over all players — a returner may since have lapsed again). Volunteer ratio: distinct admin actors in the trailing 12 months vs distinct players in the same window; the 'core' variant uses the ~5 committee+deputy roles. Right-censoring: recent lapsers have had less time to show an outcome — shares are conservative for the engager categories. All aggregates; no names. Script analysis/gP_g1.py.
