Does per-member admin load rise with size?
How few hands do the admin
Findings
  • The club grew from 43 to 81 active members/quarter (OLS +2.78/qtr, p=0.02) — a real scaling test over 10 quarters.
  • Per-member admin load does not clearly rise over the window: early 0.265 → late 0.214 actions/member, Spearman ρ=-0.224 (p=0.533). On this short series the per-member cost is not clearly rising — the club is absorbing growth without a proportional admin blow-up, at least so far.
  • But the work is done by very few hands: HHI 0.235 means an effective 4.2 admins carry all roster maintenance, out of 34 who ever acted. The scale risk is concentration, not yet volume.
  • [ASSUMPTION] All membership-event actions count as admin load (including 'removed' bulk cleanups). Excluding removals would smooth the 2025-Q1 spike but not change the flat/low per-member trend or the HHI story.
Method, data & caveats
Load per member = membership-event actions in a quarter ÷ distinct check-in persons that quarter. Window = 2024-Q1 … latest, the span where the attendance denominator exists (10 quarters). Trend tested with Spearman ρ (rank, robust to the short series) and an OLS slope for magnitude. HHI = Σ(actor share of actions)² over the 34 distinct admin actors since 2024; 1/HHI = effective number of admins; normalised HHI rescales to [0,1]. Growth confirmed by an OLS slope on active-members. No causal claim — this is a descriptive scale-vs-load shape on a small series.
