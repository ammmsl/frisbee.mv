Findings
  • The AoA reads like a national federation — grassroots into age-group national teams and international success, across all atolls, into schools. The DB shows a single-venue pickup club: ~322 people reached (roster 172 + periphery 150) and 244 tracked sessions since Jan 2024.
  • One core venue carries the whole club. 89% of tracked sessions are at Villingili; every one of the 6 tracked venues is in Greater Malé. Against an all-atoll objective, the data shows zero tracked sessions outside the capital region — the geographic promise is the widest data-anchored gap.
  • No standing national team appears in the data, and no school-club programme is visible as tracked activity — the developmental pipeline the charter describes is not yet a measurable programme, just adult pickup plus a few tournaments. This is the same means–ends decoupling B1 codes from the text, now quantified.
  • The self-reported counts don't agree with themselves: active players are variously '200+', '250+', '100+'; members '40+/50+/60'; sessions '100+/200+/1,780'. Reconciled, the DB gives ~322 reached and 244 tracked sessions — a decoupled body reports the ceremonial figure, and the ceremonial figures don't reconcile.
  • [ASSUMPTION] The tracker starts Jan 2024, so the 2018–2023 free-FAM Friday-morning era is outside the data — 'zero atoll sessions' and '244 sessions' describe the tracked era, not the club's full history. Atoll introductions (Fuvahmulah/Addu) may have happened as events not captured as tracked sessions.
Method, data & caveats
Approach. The data-anchored sibling of B1's policy-text audit: pull aggregate numbers from dist/ufa.sqlite and set them against the Articles of Association's stated objectives (grassroots → national teams → international; all-atoll; schools — GD.EVIDENCE). Sessions grouped by corrections.venue() over the sessions table (244 rows, Jan 2024 → Jun 2026); 89% at Villingili, all venues in Greater Malé. People reached = roster 172 + periphery 150 = 322, from corrections.COUNTS (deduped via the confirmed alias map — never select * from people raw). Members from people.member_2026 (aggregate count). Self-reported vs reconciled counts from GD.SELF_REPORT_CONFLICTS. DB use is aggregate only; no money, no PII, office-holders as roles. Tracked-era caveat: pre-2024 is untracked. Descriptive, not causal. Script: analysis/gD_oms5.py; substrate: analysis/gD_evidence.py, analysis/corrections.py, dist/ufa.sqlite.
