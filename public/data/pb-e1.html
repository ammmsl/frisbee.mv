Findings
  • The replication unit is measurable and small: 12 on the pitch needs ~15 committed sign-ups (no-show-adjusted), a weekly pool of ~50, and ~100 people who ever try — numbers no literature could supply (Part A §2's conclusion) and which now exist, measured from one working community.
  • Population never binds in the Maldives: even Fuvahmulah (9,166 residents, Census 2022) contains the ~100-person reach requirement dozens of times over. The binding constraints are the organising core (~5) and a bookable venue — the same two assets Part A's replication-mode cases (parkrun, CrossFit) ship via a franchise kit, and the same two UFA is weakest on at home (B4; OMS-2/OMS-4).
  • The seeds already exist, and the south is one market, not two: WFDF's Congress record lists disc activity in Malé, Fuvahmulah and Addu — so node-2 is a strengthening problem, not a cold start. Addu (25,053) and Fuvahmulah (9,166) are linked by a twice-daily 45–90-min speedboat: within Part A's travel-radius logic that pair can sustain recurring inter-community play, not just annual tournament contact — the natural first 'second league radius' of the ecosystem.
  • Gap #4, one real observation: League Season 1 ran at 5 teams — below the literature's 6–8 practical floor — lost one team mid-season and completed 60% of fixtures. A single case, but the literature has none: leagues below the floor can run, at visible cost to completion.
  • Gap #3 stays open: density-dependence cannot be fit to an ecosystem of one. What n=1 does support is the inheritance inventory above — the federated-replication reading, where legitimation arrives via the NF shell rather than via organisational density.
  • [ASSUMPTION] All derivations transplant Greater-Malé-measured rates (no-show, turnout, one-and-done) to other islands; a second node would re-measure them. Populations are now Census-2022 verified; the niche context is real too — futsal dominates the amateur field (a ~119-team national challenge), so a new node competes for the same evenings, pitches and organisers.
