Findings
  • UFA runs on one pitch it does not control: 89% of sessions and 92% of all attendance are at Villingili (venue concentration HHI 0.803, where 1.0 is a single venue). That is a textbook resource-dependence exposure — the critical resource is external and substitutable only at a cost.
  • Control at that venue is near-zero: no contract, no booking rights, tertiary customer status (bump-able for football or events), and a 5-minute ferry that itself suppresses turnout. UFA is likely the only paying non-futsal user, on a public asset FAM does not formally back for other sports.
  • The only active fallback — the Hulhumalé HDC futsal pitch — is thin: paid, futsal-sized (fewer players), and confined to a late 11pm–12am Mon/Thu slot on a short-term agreement. Two former options (Rehendhi School, Henveiru) are already gone or unusable. Substitutability is low.
  • The consolidation is recent and visible only in the tracked era: after an early-2024 scramble across 6 venues, play collapsed onto Villingili from 2024-Q2. [ASSUMPTION] The mitigations are governance ones — a booking agreement, a second contracted venue, or a demand-side buffer — not analysable from this DB; logged for the owner.
Method, data & caveats
Session and attendance counts per venue from sessions and attendance ⋈ sessions; venues normalised from the messy location strings (analysis/corrections.py). Concentration = Herfindahl–Hirschman Index over each venue's session share (Σ shareᵢ²; 1.0 = a single venue). The consolidation timeline counts sessions per quarter, Villingili vs all others. The control/security column is the owner's venue ground truth (CORRECTIONS.md §2), not a DB field — treated as qualitative context. Tracked era only: the DB starts 2024-01; the 2018–~2023 free-FAM-Malé Friday-morning era is not captured, so this describes the current single-venue regime, not the club's full venue history.
