Findings
  • The core is tiny and the middle is broad: 22 people (6%) produce half of all attendance, and the top decile produces 64% — but the top 1% produces only 11%, against Nielsen's ~90%. A physical session caps any one person at one slot per night, so pickup communities cannot be 90-9-1 at the top; instead UFA runs ~11-54-35 (top 1% / next 9% / bottom 90%) — the '9%' tier is the load-bearing layer. The ~10%-active-core heuristic from voluntary-sport research fits almost exactly.
  • Dunbar's stack appears almost literally: 15 weekly regulars (layer: 15), 50 at monthly-plus (layer: 50), 204 playing at all in 12 months (layer: 150, with membership at 56 and monthly actives ~60–70), and 350 ever-touched (layer: 500). One community, without design, occupying every layer of the canonical structure at close to the canonical sizes.
  • On the viability ladder UFA sits in the safe middle: monthly actives average ~59 (range 30–77), above the ~50 elevated-closure floor from congregation studies and inside the 100–150 European-club median when counted as the 12-month playing pool (204). The 300–800 'fewest problems per member' sweet spot is a different order of magnitude — UFA is structurally a single-Dunbar-community club, and growth beyond ~150 actives would predict a phase change (program-church transition), not more of the same.
  • Gender is just above critical mass: 35% of gendered players are women — above Kanter's ≥30% threshold below which a minority reads as tokens. SM-5's caveat stands: participation rates still lag (IRR ≈ 0.77), so the stock clears the threshold while the flow does not.
Method, data & caveats
Presence substrate: Session Input's "Actual Attendees" resolved to persons (99.4% of 5,298 appearances; gS_rsvp.presence()) — unlike the roster-only attendance table, this sees the periphery's repeat play. Identity dedup applied (people 350, roster 172). Inequality shares are session-day counts per person over 2024–26; Nielsen's 90-9-1 is quoted as contribution shares by tier. Dunbar bands are attendance-frequency cuts on the trailing 12 months (92 sessions): weekly ≥50% of sessions, monthly ≥12/year — [ASSUMPTION] analyst-set cuts, direction not sacred; the organising-core count is the committee+active-deputy roles, not presence. Monthly actives = distinct persons per calendar month. Gender exists for the roster only (SM-5's caveat); unresolved raw-name appearances (0.6%) are excluded from gender but counted in presence.
