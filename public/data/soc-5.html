Findings
  • Villingili scores 0.79/1.0 on the eight-characteristic index vs Mainland's 0.50 — Villingili behaves like a real third place; the Malé/Hulhumalé venues behave like occasional pop-ups.
  • The gap is widest on accessibility & the regulars: Villingili runs 7.5 sessions/month with 73% returning ≥3×, vs Mainland's 0.9/month and 54%.
  • Both venues score high on neutral ground / leveller — UFA waves in guests, kids and first-timers free everywhere (87% free on Mainland, 13% on Villingili). Inclusiveness is a club-wide norm, not a venue effect.
  • 'Home away from home' is Villingili-specific: its top-10% of attenders account for 45% of all check-ins — a devoted core that owns the ground.
Method, data & caveats
Oldenburg's 8 third-place characteristics scored with behavioural proxies from sessions and attendance, split by venue-group (case when location='Villingili' then 'Villingili' else 'Mainland' end). Proxies: neutral ground = non-member check-in share; leveller = free (cost=0) check-in share; conversation = avg visits/person (repeat contact); accessibility = sessions/month × weekday spread; regulars = share of attenders with ≥3 visits; low profile = 1−coefficient of variation of the session-gap (steady rhythm); playful mood = session count; home-away = share of check-ins from the top-10% most-frequent attenders. Each normalised 0–1 against a fixed ceiling, then the venue index = the mean of the 8. [ASSUMPTION] "Mainland" pools all non-Villingili venues (they're individually tiny, 3–16 sessions each). [ASSUMPTION] proxy ceilings are chosen for cross-venue comparability, not calibrated externally — read rank, not level. [ASSUMPTION] chat isn't venue-tagged, so "conversation" uses repeat-visit contact. Small-n: Mainland is 26 sessions / 70 people — noisy; shape only. No individuals named. Script: analysis/gC_soc5.py.
